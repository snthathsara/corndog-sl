# CornDogSL — Official Web Experience

An editorial, high-energy street food web experience crafted for **CornDogSL (한국 핫도그 🇱🇰 🇰🇷)**, Sri Lanka's pioneer authentic Korean Corn Dog destination with outlets at **59 Marine Drive, Bambalapitiya** and **30 Galle Road, Moratuwa**.

**Cheesy Korean Corn Dogs & Street Food | Epic 100% Mozzarella Pulls | Korean Yangnyeom Bites & Cheesy Tteokbokki | Dalgona Lattes & Fruit Sparklers | Established 2021**

---

## ✨ Design & Brand Highlights

- **Signature Light Orangish Yellow & Black-ish Brown Palette**:
  - Warm light orangish yellow & golden crispy crust tones (`#F5A825`, `#FFBA33`, `#FEE9B8`, `#FFF7E6`), deep roasted espresso black-ish brown (`#140E0A`, `#1E150F`, `#241810`), and molten cheese accents.
  - Seamless Light and Dark modes inspired directly by the official CornDogSL circular badge logo.
- **Editorial Typography & Visual Hierarchy**:
  - Chunky retro display serif powered by **Fraunces** with stacked repeating typography.
  - "Crispy, cheesy & loaded with Seoul street food soul" typographic quote ribbon.
  - "IT'S CORNDOG O'CLOCK" graphic banner.
  - Transparent signature Korean Corn Dog cutout with an epic mozzarella stretch floating dynamically over the hero headline.
- **Instagram Story Highlights Bar**:
  - Korean Corn Dogs 🧀, Street Snacks 🍗, Korean Coffee ☕, Fruit Drinks 🍓 with interactive scroll-to triggers.
- **Crafted 4-3-4-3 Menu Structure**:
  - **Mains (4)**: Full Mozzarella Corn Dog (Rs. 1,450), Potato Mozzarella Corn Dog - Gamja (Rs. 1,650), Half & Half Corn Dog (Rs. 1,350), Crispy Ramen Crusted Corn Dog (Rs. 1,550).
  - **Snacks & Street Bites (3)**: Korean Yangnyeom Chicken Bites (Rs. 1,750), Cheesy Tteokbokki (Rs. 1,850), Kimchi Cheese Loaded Fries (Rs. 1,400).
  - **Korean Coffee & Handcrafted Sips (4)**: Korean Dalgona Honeycomb Latte (Rs. 1,150), Iced Korean Americano - Ah-Ah (Rs. 850), Brown Sugar Bingsu Latte (Rs. 1,250), Condensed Milk Iced Kopi (Rs. 980).
  - **Korean Refreshers & Fruit Drinks (3)**: Korean Fresh Strawberry Milk - Ttalgi-uyu (Rs. 1,100), Sparkling Yuzu Citrus Cooler (Rs. 950), Iced Peach Oolong Tea (Rs. 900).
- **Live Restaurant Status Engine (Dual Outlets)**:
  - Automatically calculates Sri Lanka time (`Asia/Colombo`) and displays real-time open/closed status for both branches:
    - **Bambalapitiya (59, Marine Drive)**: Mon–Sun 4:00 PM – 11:00 PM.
    - **Moratuwa (30, Galle Road)**: Tue–Sun 2:00 PM – 9:30 PM (Closed Mondays).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Markup & Styling**: Semantic HTML5, Modular Modern CSS (CSS Variables, clamp(), Flexbox, CSS Grid)
- **Tooling & Bundler**: [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Typography**: Google Fonts (Fraunces, Inter, JetBrains Mono)
- **Icons**: Custom Handcrafted SVGs & Lucide
